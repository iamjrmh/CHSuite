Requirements:
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- Python 3.11 (64-bit) - from [python.org](https://python.org), check "Add Python to PATH" during install

1. To Generate themes, just double click the ThemeGen.py. It will open a simple gui for live editing of the CHSuite theme.
2. Mess around with the colors and find something you like! Once you do, export it to the ```/themes/``` folder.
3. Send them to your friends! They're able to imported into an already existing build of the CHSuite by placing the ```theme.json``` inside of your CHSuite /themes/ folder ```(ex. C:\CHSuite\themes)```
